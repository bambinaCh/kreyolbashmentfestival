Accessibility

This page describes our intentions. It is not a formal conformance statement.

This website

We aim to make this website usable for as many people as possible. It is designed with keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient colour contrast, descriptive labels for form fields and support for reduced-motion settings in mind.

If you encounter a barrier on this website, please let us know via the contact section so we can improve it.

The festival

The festival is planned primarily in the outdoor area of GZ Wollishofen. Mobile toilets, including an accessible toilet, are planned. Detailed accessibility information about the venue will be published closer to the festival.

If you have specific needs, please get in touch with us – we are happy to help plan your visit.
