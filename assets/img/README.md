# Image specifications

Every image slot on the site is currently a neutral placeholder. The label on each
placeholder shows what belongs there and the intended size. Recommended format:
**WebP or AVIF** (JPEG is fine), logos as **SVG**. Keep photos under ~300 KB.

| Slot | Folder | Size (px) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| KBF logo (header, footer) | `brand/` | 400 × 160 (SVG preferred) | 5:2 |
| Favicon | `brand/` | SVG or 512 × 512 PNG | 1:1 |
| Hero background | `hero/` | 2400 × 1350 | 16:9 |
| About – Caribbean community | `hero/` or `gallery/` | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 |
| Experience – Music | `gallery/` | 1600 × 1000 | 16:10 |
| Experience – Food, Drinks, Culture, Community, Market | `gallery/` | 1200 × 900 | 4:3 |
| Artist portraits | `artists/` | 1200 × 1500 | 4:5 |
| Food mosaic – dish | `vendors/` | 1000 × 1250 | 4:5 |
| Food mosaic – grill, fruit | `vendors/` | 1000 × 1000 | 1:1 |
| Vendor cards | `vendors/` | 1200 × 800 | 3:2 |
| Bar & drinks | `vendors/` | 1500 × 1000 | 3:2 |
| Map / site plan | `hero/` | 1600 × 1000 | 16:10 |
| Venue, outdoor area | `gallery/` | 1200 × 900 | 4:3 |
| Volunteer crew (feature) | `gallery/` | 1600 × 1200 | 4:3 |
| Get Involved cards | `gallery/` | 1200 × 800 | 3:2 |
| GZ Wollishofen logo (**only with approval**) | `partners/` | 500 × 200 (SVG preferred) | 5:2 |
| Partner / sponsor logos | `partners/` | 500 × 200 (SVG/PNG) | 5:2 |
| Gallery – tall | `gallery/` | 1200 × 1600 | 3:4 |
| Gallery – wide | `gallery/` | 1600 × 900 | 16:9 |
| Gallery – square | `gallery/` | 1200 × 1200 | 1:1 |
| Social share image (Open Graph) | `og/` | 1200 × 630 | 1.91:1 |

Only use images you have the rights to, and get consent from recognisable people.
