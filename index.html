    About KBF

    More than a festival. A celebration of who we are.

    Kreyol Bashment Festival – Caribbean Fever brings the warmth of the Caribbean to a summer afternoon and night in Zürich: sound systems and dance floors, the smell of the grill, languages crossing the courtyard and old friends finding each other in the crowd.

    We celebrate the full breadth of the Caribbean – the Spanish-speaking, French-speaking, English-speaking and Creole-speaking islands and their diaspora – through music, food, dance and togetherness. KBF is a meeting place for the Caribbean community in Switzerland and for everyone curious about it: families and children, groups of friends, music lovers, food fans and neighbours from across Zürich.

    KBF is organised by Kreyol Bashment Entertainment as a community-driven project, not a commercial one. Any surplus goes back into future editions. Our ambition: to grow Kreyol Bashment Festival into an annual Caribbean cultural event in Zürich.

    Calypso culture

    Where the carnival found its voice.

    Born in Trinidad & Tobago, calypso turned everyday life, politics and humour into song – sharp lyrics, big wit and a rhythm made for the road. Soca grew out of it, and calypso is still the storytelling heart of carnival across the Caribbean. At KBF we celebrate that spirit: music that makes you move and makes you listen.

    Discover the flavours of the Caribbean.

    Community & sustainability

    Different roots. Shared rhythms.

    A good party leaves people closer – and leaves the place as we found it. These are the principles we're planning KBF around.

    People & community

    • Community participation. Volunteers, local vendors and artists shape the festival together.
    • Cultural diversity. We celebrate the many languages, islands and identities of the Caribbean.
    • Inclusion. Everyone is welcome, regardless of background, age or ability.
    • Respectful behaviour. No room for discrimination, harassment or disrespect.
    • Responsible celebration. Enjoy yourself, look out for each other, drink responsibly.

    Place & environment

    • Waste separation. We are planning for waste to be sorted on site.
    • Reusable materials. We aim to use reusable materials wherever possible.
    • Care for the grounds. We treat the festival grounds and the neighbourhood with respect.
    • Long-term thinking. Any surplus supports future editions and the development of the project.
